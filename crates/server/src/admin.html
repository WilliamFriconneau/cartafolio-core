Administration

Each person has a memory of their own: their own vaults, keys and connections. Nobody reads another's. An account deleted in Home Assistant leaves its person here, with their memory: nothing is ever erased for you.

Safety: an heir, a recovery key

If your Home Assistant account is deleted, your memory is not: it stays, and nothing is ever erased for you. Two ways to get it back, both yours to set up.

Your heir

A recovery key

Shown once: keep it somewhere safe, a password manager or a paper. A new Home Assistant account that enters it on the waiting page takes your memory over. A new key cancels the old one.
